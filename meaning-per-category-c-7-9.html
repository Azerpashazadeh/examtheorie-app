Meaning per Category — C

Category C signs indicate closures (no entry), one-way streets, and rush hour lane openings.

🚫 Core Closure and One-Way Signs

C-1
Closed in Both Directions

Closed to vehicles, horse riders, and handlers of horses and livestock. Pedestrians are allowed.

C-2
One-Way Street — No Entry from This Side

Closed in this direction to vehicles. Pedestrians allowed from this side. Also placed at end of motorway/autoweg deceleration lanes in the wrong direction — always accompanied by a "ga terug" (go back) plate.

C-3
One-Way Street — Entry from This Side Only

Paired with C-2 at the other end of a one-way street. If C-2 is at the start, C-3 is at the end.

C-4
One-Way Street on the Right

Seen at T-junctions. You must turn right.

C-5
Entry from Both Sides Allowed

Seen near roads that look one-way (e.g. parallel roads alongside main roads) to confirm they are not one-way.

C-2 at motorway/autoweg deceleration lane exits is always combined with a "ga terug" (go back) supplementary plate to prevent drivers entering in the wrong direction.

📌 C-6 to C-22 follow the same pattern as C-1

Signs C-6 through C-22 are the same as C-1 but specific to a particular group. The group is indicated by an image, number, or letters on the sign.

🌿 C-22 Environmental Zone — Supplementary Plates

Environmental zone signs may be accompanied by a supplementary plate showing the emission class from which diesel vehicles are permitted. Higher class = cleaner vehicle.

C-22a3
Accessible to passenger & commercial cars of emission class 5 and up
C-22a4
Accessible to lorries of emission class 4 and up
C-22a9
Accessible to lorries and buses of emission class 6 and up

🟢 Rush Hour Lane Signs — C-23

The last C-series signs are not closures — they regulate the opening and closing of extra (rush hour) lanes.

C-23-01
Rush hour lane open
C-23-02
Clear rush hour lane
C-23-03
End of rush hour lane

🚍 Destination Traffic and Supplementary Plates

📌 Destination Traffic

Drivers whose destination is on the closed road and who cannot reach it via any other route. Public buses are always classed as destination traffic.

C-6 — Destination Traffic Exempt

The supplementary plate indicates destination traffic may always enter — even if they fall in the closed category (motor vehicles on more than two wheels).

C-12 — During Certain Hours

The plate shows the times the sign applies. Outside these hours, the road is open to all traffic, including the closed category (all motor vehicles).
