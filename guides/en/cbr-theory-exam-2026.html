2026 Dutch CBR Car Theory Exam Guide: 50 Questions, Costs & Passing Score

The Dutch CBR overhauled the passenger-car theory exam (Rijbewijs B) into a single continuous 50-question exam with animated traffic clips. Here is everything you need to know about the 2026 format, official fees, languages, and how to pass on your first attempt.

1. How the New Integrated 50-Question CBR Exam Works

Previously, the CBR car theory test was split into separate blocks (25 static hazard perception photos with a strict 8-second timer, 12 knowledge questions, and 28 insight questions) where failing a single block meant failing the entire test. Under the current integrated CBR structure, all topics are woven into one continuous exam of 50 scored questions in 30 minutes (plus 2 unscored trial questions used by CBR for quality testing).

2026 Passing Score: You must answer at least 44 out of 50 questions correctly (88%). That means you are allowed a maximum of 6 mistakes across the entire exam.
  • Dynamic Animated Videos: Static hazard photos have been replaced with short animated video situations viewed from the driver’s seat so you can judge moving cyclists, pedestrians, speed changes, and blind spots realistically.
  • Flexible Time Budget: You receive 30 minutes total for the whole exam (an average of ~36 seconds per question) and can review or navigate calmly without an 8-second panic countdown.
  • Question Formats Tested: Single-choice (A/B/C), multiple-answer checkboxes, drag-and-drop priority ordering, click-the-hazard/image selection, yes/no questions, and numeric input (e.g. speed limit in km/h).

4. How Long Is Your CBR Theory Certificate Valid?

Once you pass, your CBR theory result is registered electronically and remains valid for exactly 1.5 years (18 months) from the pass date. You must pass your practical driving test within those 18 months; CBR cannot extend an expired theory certificate under any circumstances.

How many mistakes can you make on the 2026 CBR car theory exam?

You can make a maximum of 6 mistakes: you need at least 44 correct answers out of 50 scored questions (88%) within 30 minutes.

In 2026, the official CBR car theory exam in English costs €57.00 (€50.50 base tariff plus €6.50 English surcharge) when booked directly at Mijn CBR with DigiD.

How long is the Dutch driving theory certificate valid?

Your passed CBR theory certificate is valid for 1.5 years (18 months). You must complete and pass your practical driving test before it expires.
