Speed & Safety · Updated for 2026 CBR & RVV Rules

Dutch Speed Limits, Motorways (100/130 km/h), Alcohol & Environmental Zones (2026)

Speed limits, motorway rules, environmental zones, and alcohol/drug thresholds appear in almost every 2026 CBR car theory exam. This public summary of Chapters 3 and 4 covers every exact number you must memorize.

1. Complete 2026 Dutch Speed Limit Table (Passenger Cars & Trailers)

Unless traffic signs or overhead electronic matrix panels indicate a lower limit, the statutory RVV 1990 speed limits in the Netherlands are:

CBR Exam Trap: A speed painted on the asphalt alone is only a reminder — legal speed limits come from road type, RVV signs (A1), or overhead electronic matrix signs (which always override roadside A1 signs when lower!).

2. Motorway Rules, Rush-Hour Lanes (Spitsstrook) & Hard Shoulder

  • Minimum Entry Speed Requirement: To use an autoweg (G3), your vehicle must be legally capable of and allowed to drive at least 50 km/h; on an autosnelweg (G1), at least 60 km/h (there is no fixed statutory minimum driving speed in congestion, provided you do not endanger traffic under Article 5 WVW).
  • Hard Shoulder (Vluchtstrook): Use ONLY in genuine emergencies (breakdown or acute medical crisis) — never to make a phone call, check navigation, or let a passenger out.
  • Rush-Hour Lane (Spitsstrook): When green overhead arrows open a rush-hour lane on the right, you may (and should, under the keep-right rule) cross the solid white edge line to drive there. A red X means the lane is strictly closed.

3. Environmental Zones (Milieuzone) & Zero-Emission Zones (2026)

Many Dutch cities enforce clean-air zones signed with C22a (Milieuzone, primarily restricting older diesel cars below Euro 4/5 depending on municipality) and C22c (Zero-emissiezone for commercial vans and lorries). Passenger cars running on petrol, hybrid, LPG, or electric power are currently unaffected by standard diesel passenger-car milieuzones, but always read the emission sub-plate beneath the sign.

4. Alcohol (0.2‰ vs 0.5‰), Drugs, Medication & Fatigue

  • Beginner Drivers (Beginnersrijbewijs — first 5 years, or 7 years if obtained at age 17): Maximum 0.2‰ blood alcohol (88 µg/l breath) — less than one standard glass!
  • Experienced Drivers (>5 years): Maximum 0.5‰ blood alcohol (220 µg/l breath).
  • Medication Stickers & Drugs: Driving under yellow/red warning-label medication that impairs reaction time, or illegal drugs (zero tolerance; combining drugs + alcohol multiplies crash risk exponentially), is a criminal offence under Article 8 WVW.
  • 2-Second Following Distance: Maintain at least 2 seconds of distance in dry weather (more in rain, fog, or behind lorries) and take a 15-minute break every 2 hours of continuous driving.

Frequently Asked Questions

When can you drive 130 km/h in the Netherlands?

Only on motorways (autosnelwegen, sign G1) between 19:00 and 06:00 where no 100 km/h or 120 km/h sign restricts the speed. Between 06:00 and 19:00 the nationwide motorway limit is 100 km/h.

How fast may a car with a caravan drive on a Dutch motorway?

Up to 90 km/h if the trailer or caravan has a maximum authorized mass up to 3,500 kg (80 km/h if over 3,500 kg).

What is the alcohol limit for new drivers in the Netherlands?

0.2 promille (88 µg/l breath) during the first 5 years after obtaining your first driving licence (7 years if obtained before age 18).
