Dutch Vehicle Rules, APK Inspection, Car Lights, Tyres & Road Traffic Law (2026)

Every CBR car theory exam tests technical vehicle safety, lighting rules, load/trailer limits, and the Dutch Road Traffic Act (Wegenverkeerswet 1994). Here is the complete public cheat-sheet for Chapters 5 through 8.

1. Car Lighting Rules: Dipped Beam, Fog Lights (<50 m vs <200 m) & Dashboard Warnings

  • Dipped Headlights (Dimlicht): Mandatory at night and during daytime whenever visibility is poor (rain, fog, snow, tunnels). Daytime running lights (DRL) do NOT illuminate your tail lights in many cars!
  • Front Fog Lights (Mistvoorlichten): Allowed ONLY when fog, snowfall, or heavy rain seriously restricts visibility (generally under 200 metres).
  • Rear Fog Light (Mistachterlicht): Allowed ONLY during fog or heavy snowfall when visibility is less than 50 metres — strictly forbidden in rain alone because reflection dazzles drivers behind you!
  • Dashboard Warning Lights: A RED warning light (oil pressure, brakes, battery/alternator, coolant temperature) means stop safely immediately and switch off the engine. An AMBER/YELLOW light (engine check, tyre pressure TPMS, low washer fluid) means check or visit a workshop soon.

2. Tyres (1.6 mm), Mirrors, APK Inspection Schedule & Mandatory Documents

Requirement2026 Dutch Legal Rule (Rijbewijs B)
Minimum Tyre Tread Depth1.6 mm across all main grooves (winter replacement recommended around 4 mm)

Mandatory Documents When DrivingValid physical Driving Licence + Registration Card (Kentekenbewijs) + minimum WA Third-Party Liability Insurance registered in RDW

3. Trailers (750 kg Rule), White vs Yellow Number Plates & Protruding Loads

  • Licence Category B Trailer Limit: With a standard B licence you may tow any trailer up to 750 kg authorized mass, OR a heavier trailer provided the combined maximum authorized mass (car + trailer) does not exceed 3,500 kg.
  • Number Plates on Trailers: Trailers ≤750 kg carry a white repeater plate matching the towing car; trailers >750 kg have their own registration and a yellow number plate + independent breakaway cable/auxiliary coupling.
  • Load Projection: Roof/car loads may never project past the front bumper of a passenger car, and at most 1.00 metre behind the rear axle/vehicle (up to max 5 m behind rear axle on trailers with a red-and-white marker board if >1 m). Width on paved roads max 2.55 m.

4. Road Traffic Act (Art. 5 & 6 WVW), Beginner Points & Special Convoys

  • Article 5 WVW: General duty never to cause danger or unnecessary hindrance on the road. Article 6 WVW: Causing a serious injury or fatal crash through culpable/reckless driving (criminal offence).
  • Beginner Licence (2 Strikes): Serious traffic offences (tailgating, >30 km/h speeding, >40 km/h on motorways, causing danger) earn penalty points; at 2 points within 5 years, your licence is suspended for a mandatory CBR driving competence investigation.
  • Emergency Vehicles & Military Columns: Always yield immediately to vehicles using both blue flashing lights AND a two-tone siren. Never cut through a military motor convoy (first vehicle: two blue flags/lights; intermediate: one blue; final vehicle: one green) once the lead vehicle has entered an equal intersection or traffic light!

Frequently Asked Questions

May you use your rear fog light during heavy rain on the motorway?

No! In the Netherlands, the rear fog light is permitted ONLY in fog or heavy snow when visibility is below 50 metres. Using it in rain is illegal.

How often does a petrol or electric car need an APK inspection in the Netherlands?

Following the 4-2-2-1 rule: 4 years after first registration, then after 2 years, another 2 years, and annually thereafter.

When does a trailer need its own yellow registration plate?

Whenever the trailer’s maximum authorized mass (toegestane maximummassa) exceeds 750 kg. Up to 750 kg it uses a white plate with the towing car’s registration.
