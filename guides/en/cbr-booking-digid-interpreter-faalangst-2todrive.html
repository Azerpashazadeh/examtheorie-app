How to Book Your CBR Theory Exam with DigiD: Foreign Languages, Interpreter, Extra Time & 2toDrive (2026)

Many candidates accidentally overpay €30–€60 on unofficial booking websites or wonder how to take the CBR exam in Turkish, Arabic, Russian, Persian, or Spanish. Here is the official 2026 booking and exam-options guide.

1. How to Book Official CBR Theory Exam Directly via Mijn CBR (Avoid Broker Fees!)

Commercial third-party websites often rank in Google Ads and charge €80–€110 for a €50.50 exam. Always book directly on the official government portal mijn.cbr.nl:

  1. Log in at mijn.cbr.nl with your personal DigiD (via the DigiD app).
  2. Choose Theorie-examen reserveren → Personenauto (Rijbewijs B).
  3. Select language (Nederlands €50.50 or Engels €57.00), your preferred CBR exam centre (e.g. Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht, Eindhoven, Rijswijk, Arnhem, Zwolle), date, and time slot, then pay via iDEAL.
  4. Need to change your date? On Mijn CBR you can move your appointment free of charge up to 7 days before the exam (or take an earlier slot if one opens).

2. Taking the CBR Exam in Turkish, Arabic, Russian, Persian, or Spanish (Tolkennet Interpreter)

Can I take the CBR exam in my own language? Standard touchscreen exams in the group hall are available ONLY in Dutch and English. However, if you prefer Turkish, Arabic, Russian, Persian (Farsi), Spanish, Polish, Ukrainian, or another language, you have two smart strategies:
  • Option A (Most Popular & Cheapest — €57): Study all Dutch RVV rules and 50 realistic exams in your native language + English on ExamTheorie so you understand every trap 100%, then sit the standard English touchscreen exam at Mijn CBR for just €57.00.
  • Option B (Individueel Begeleid Examen + Official Interpreter): Book an Individueel begeleid examen (individual supervised exam) through CBR (or your driving school) and order a certified interpreter via Tolkennet at least 14 days in advance. A CBR employee sits with you in a private room while the sworn interpreter translates each question and answer choice aloud. (Family/friends are never permitted to translate.)

3. Extra-Time Exam (Theorie-examen Extra Tijd) & Anxiety / Dyslexia Support

Candidates with dyslexia, ADHD, reading difficulties, or severe test anxiety can book the Theorie-examen met extra tijd directly on Mijn CBR (no medical certificate required!). You receive 15 extra minutes (45 minutes total instead of 30) and free audio read-aloud via headphones in Dutch or English. For the practical driving test, CBR also offers a dedicated Faalangst-examen (80 minutes including a calming time-out option).

4. 2toDrive: Theory at 16, Driving Lessons at 16.5 & Practical Exam at 17

  • From 16 years old: You may sit the official CBR car theory exam (certificate valid for 1.5 years).
  • From 16.5 years old: You may start practical driving lessons and apply for your begeleiderspas (accompanying coach pass, naming 1 to 5 experienced coaches aged 27+ with 5+ years of clean driving licence).
  • From 17 years old: Pass your CBR practical test and drive accompanied by your registered coach until your 18th birthday, after which you drive independently!

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I avoid overpaying when booking my CBR theory exam?

Only book at the official portal mijn.cbr.nl using your DigiD and iDEAL (€50.50 Dutch / €57.00 English). Avoid commercial intermediary sites at the top of search ads.

Can I take the CBR theory exam with a Turkish, Arabic, Russian, Persian, or Spanish interpreter?

Yes. You book an Individueel begeleid examen at CBR and book a sworn interpreter exclusively through Tolkennet at least 2 weeks before your exam date.

Do I need a doctor’s note to book the CBR Extra-Time theory exam?

No medical note is needed for the Extra-Time theory exam: anyone who wants 15 extra minutes and audio headphones can select it directly on Mijn CBR for a small CBR surcharge.
