Supplementary Plates

Supplementary plates are placed beneath signs to clarify, limit, or extend the sign's meaning.

🚗 Vehicle Category Plates — OB-01 to OB-14

OB-01
Only applies to horse riders
OB-02
Only applies to cyclists
OB-03
Only applies to moped riders
OB-04
Only applies to cyclists and moped riders
OB-05
Only applies to agricultural vehicles
OB-06
Only applies to motorcyclists
OB-07
Only applies to motorcyclists and motorists
OB-08
Only applies to motor vehicles with more than two wheels
OB-09
Only applies to motorists (passenger car drivers)
OB-10
Only applies to vehicles with a trailer
OB-11
Only applies to lorry drivers
OB-12
Only applies to bus drivers
OB-13
Only applies to lorry and bus drivers
OB-14
Only applies to tram drivers
🔄 "Uitgezonderd" — Excepted

Any OB plate can also be used with the word "uitgezonderd" (excepted). In that case, the sign above does not apply to the displayed category — it applies to all other categories instead.

🔶 Priority Sign Supplementary Plates

📌 How these work

These plates are used with priority signs. The thick line indicates the divergent priority road. The thin lines indicate the side roads. Drivers on side roads must give right of way to drivers on the priority road.
