Meaning per Category — H

This small but important category contains just two signs: H-1 (begin built-up area) and H-2 (end of built-up area). Because these signs change several traffic rules, knowing them is essential.

🪧 Sign Variants

The H-1 and H-2 signs come in several variants. All have the same legal meaning.

⚠️ Neighbourhood Sign — No Legal Status

Neighbourhood Sign — looks like H-1 but has NO legal meaning

After this sign you are not in a built-up area. Used for neighbourhoods without a built-up area designation, or large municipalities with buildings outside the actual built-up area. The 50 km/h limit and other H-1 rules do not apply here.
