Roundabouts

A roundabout is a special intersection. It consists of a circular main road with a number of side roads. The direction of travel is always counterclockwise. The traffic rules on roundabouts are the same as at an intersection — it depends on whether or not there are signs how the priority is arranged.

⚠️
No Signs — Right from Right

Without signs, drivers from the right have priority. Drivers already on the roundabout must give way to drivers entering. This is uncommon today.

🔵
With B-6 Signs — Roundabout Has Priority

At most roundabouts, B-6 signs and priority triangles control priority. Drivers going around have priority over drivers entering.

At most roundabouts, road signs govern right of way. When approaching a roundabout, this is usually the B-6 sign. There are almost always priority triangles on the road surface. In this case, drivers going around have priority over drivers entering.

B-6
Give right of way to drivers on the intersecting road
D-1
Roundabout — compulsory direction of travel (counterclockwise)

Leaving the Roundabout

Leaving a roundabout is seen as a right turn. You must indicate in time before leaving. Also give way to through traffic on the same road (the roundabout itself) — including cyclists, snorfietsers, and pedestrians.

Cyclists on Separate Paths

At some roundabouts, cyclists, snorfietsers, and pedestrians do not take part in roundabout traffic. In these cases, cycle paths are further away from the roundabout and do not bend and turn with it.

ℹ Where a cycle path or footpath crosses the road, this is treated as a separate intersection. Usually the cycle path has sign B-6 and priority triangles. If you leave the roundabout, you do not have to give way to snorfietsers, cyclists, and pedestrians on such a separate path.

Turbo Roundabout

A roundabout can also be designed as a multi-lane roundabout. Most new multi-lane roundabouts are "turbo roundabouts" — a spiral design where choosing the right lane at the beginning allows you to leave at the correct exit. Changing lanes on the roundabout itself is often not possible.

💡 On the lead road to a turbo roundabout, arrows on the road surface indicate which lane to take for which direction. Choose your lane in time — it is often not possible to change once on the roundabout.
