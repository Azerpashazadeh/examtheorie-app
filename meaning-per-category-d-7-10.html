Meaning per Category — D

Category D signs indicate which driving direction you must follow, or on which side you may or must pass the sign. They apply to all drivers unless otherwise stated.

🔵 Passing and Roundabout Signs

D-1
Roundabout — Mandatory Direction

Found on roundabouts. You must drive counterclockwise.

D-2
Pass on the Right (or Left)

You must pass the sign on the right. The arrow can also indicate the left side depending on placement.

D-3
Pass on Either Side

The sign may be passed on both sides. Usually placed on a centre guide — a traffic island or obstacle between two lanes.

➡️ Mandatory Direction of Travel — D-4 to D-7

These signs indicate a mandatory direction of travel without indicating a one-way street. They can occur in all directions — left, right, straight, and combinations.

D-4
Straight on
D-5
Right
D-6
Straight on and right
D-7
Left and right
📌 Not a one-way street

D-4 to D-7 signs indicate a mandatory direction at that point only. They do not turn the road into a one-way street.

🚲 Cyclist and Moped Direction Signs — D-101 to D-104

These signs are specifically for cyclists (and snorfietsers) or moped riders (and speed-pedelec drivers). They are placed where these road users must enter or leave the road and join the cycle or cycle/moped path.

D-101
Cyclists (and snorfietsers) must pass on the right
D-102
Cyclists (and snorfietsers) must pass on the left
D-103
Mopeds must pass on the right
D-104
Mopeds must pass on the left
