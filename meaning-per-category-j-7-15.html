Meaning per Category — J

Category J consists entirely of warning signs. They give you the opportunity to anticipate danger in time. They can be combined with category A signs (speed limit or recommended speed).

✖️ St. Andrew's Crosses — J-12 and J-13

St. Andrew's crosses are always displayed at level crossings (J-10 and J-11). They show how many tracks cross the road.

J-12
Single St. Andrew's Cross

One track only. After a train has passed, you may proceed.

J-13
Double St. Andrew's Cross

Two or more tracks. After one train passes, a second train can come from the opposite direction.

🖥️ Dynamic & Matrix Sign Warnings

📡 Typically used on electronic matrix signs

These signs appear when the situation arises. They can also be permanent in places where the situation regularly occurs — e.g. traffic jam hotspots or viaducts with frequent crosswind.

J-31
Crosswind
J-33
Possible traffic jam
J-34
Accident
J-35
Reduced visibility: snow, rain or fog
J-36
Sleet or snow

🔺 General Danger Sign — J-37

When none of the specific J signs represent the correct danger, J-37 is used as a general danger sign. The specific danger is described on a supplementary plate below the sign.

J-37
Danger: rutting
J-37
Danger: exit construction
