Meaning per Category — B

Category B signs regulate priority — right of way. They cover both roads where you have priority and situations where you must give priority.

🔶 Priority Road Signs — B-1 to B-5

B-1
Priority Road

Drivers on side roads must give you right of way. Placed just before intersections inside built-up areas, and just after intersections outside built-up areas.

B-2
End of Priority Road

After this sign, you must give right of way to drivers approaching from the right.

B-3
Priority Intersection

Drivers on side roads must give right of way to drivers travelling on this road.

B-4
Priority Intersection — Side Road on the Left

Drivers on the side road to the left must give right of way to drivers on this road.

B-5
Priority Intersection — Side Road on the Right

Drivers on the side road to the right must give right of way to drivers on this road.

🚶 Pedestrians — important note

Priority signs B-1 to B-5 are only intended for drivers, not for pedestrians. Pedestrians on these roads do not have right of way over drivers.

🔻 Give-Way Signs — B-6 and B-7

B-6
Give Right of Way

You must give right of way to drivers on the intersecting road. You are approaching a priority road or priority intersection. You do not have to stop — but you must give way.

B-7 — STOP
Stop and Give Right of Way

Same meaning as B-6, but you must always come to a complete stop here before giving right of way to drivers on the intersecting road.

🚶 Pedestrians at B-6 and B-7

B-6 and B-7 direct you to give right of way to drivers only. Pedestrians approaching from the left or right at these intersections do not have right of way over you.
