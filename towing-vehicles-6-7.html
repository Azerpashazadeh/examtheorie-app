Towing Vehicles

If a vehicle is no longer functioning, it may be towed by another vehicle. Towing can be dangerous and is subject to a number of rules.

📋 Towing Rules
  • You can only tow one other motor vehicle with a motor vehicle.
  • You may not tow a two-wheeled motor vehicle, and you may not be towed by a two-wheeled motor vehicle.
  • The distance between the two vehicles must not exceed 5 metres.
  • No persons may be seated in the towed vehicle, except the driver and passengers who no longer fit in the front vehicle.
  • The towed vehicle must use its hazard lights to warn other road users.
5m
Maximum tow distance

The distance between the towing and towed vehicle may never exceed 5 metres. This leaves very little room to brake — drive slowly and avoid busy roads, the autoweg, and the motorway.

🪪 Driving Licence Required

The driver of the towed vehicle is still considered to be operating a motor vehicle. A valid driving licence for that vehicle is therefore required.

🚛 Trailers

The towed vehicle is also treated as a trailer and is subject to all rules applicable to trailers. This means the driver of the front vehicle must follow the rules for driving a vehicle with a trailer.

⚠️ Points of Attention While Towing

📏
Short distance to the pulling vehicle

With only 5 metres between vehicles, there is little room to brake when the front vehicle brakes. Drive slowly, avoid difficult and busy roads, and avoid the autoweg and motorway.

〰️
Maintain tension on the towline

The most force is placed on the cable when it suddenly re-tensions after going slack. Keep the cable as tensioned as possible throughout the journey.

🔑
Account for steering lock

Many passenger cars lock the steering wheel when the ignition is off and the key is removed. Leave the key in the ignition and turn it one position so the steering wheel remains unlocked.

🛑
Account for missing power steering and brakes

Power-assisted brakes and steering will not function when the engine is off. Braking and steering in the towed vehicle will therefore require significantly more effort.

🔆
Hazard lights

The towed vehicle must use hazard lights throughout. Without them, following and oncoming traffic cannot tell that a tow is in progress.

⚡
Electric vehicles

Most electric vehicles cannot be towed. Because they are designed to generate electricity through the wheels, towing can overload the electrical circuit. Always check the instruction booklet. In most cases, an electric car must be transported on a trailer or car transporter in the event of a breakdown.

⚡ Electric Vehicle Warning

Never assume an electric vehicle can be towed in the usual way. Always check the owner's manual first. Towing an electric car that is not designed for it can cause serious damage to the electrical system.
