Driving with a Trailer

If you obtain the driving licence for the passenger car, you may also drive it with a (light) trailer. This combination is sometimes subject to different rules.

Speed Limit

The speed limit with a trailer behind your passenger car is almost the same as without one — except on autowegen and motorways.

A passenger car with a trailer may never drive faster than 90 km/h on the autoweg or on the motorway.

If a lower speed is indicated on these roads, that lower speed applies.

🛣️ Normal roads — same as passenger car
🚗🔗 Autoweg / Motorway — max 90 km/h
⚠ Lower sign posted? Follow that instead

Road Section

The appropriate road section for a passenger car with a trailer is the same as for those without trailers. However, on the motorway there is one important exception:

With a combination longer than 7 metres on the motorway, you may not use a lane other than the two lanes on the far right.

Because a combination of a passenger car and trailer quickly exceeds seven metres, this rule generally applies to all these combinations. This rule also applies to all lorries.

💡 If you need to drive in a different lane to pre-sort, it is allowed to do so. On a motorway with a trailer you are seen as slower traffic — use the right lane as much as possible for the direction you want to go.
