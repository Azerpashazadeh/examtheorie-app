Speed Limits

Speed limits are usually indicated on road signs. However, there are a number of scenarios where driving at the speed limit is not possible. Drivers are expected to keep in mind:

The nature and condition of the road (speed-bumps, potholes, etc.); weather conditions such as rain and fog; the condition of the vehicle (e.g. winter tyres in winter); and how busy the roads are.

You must be able to adjust your speed and following distance when necessary and be able to stop your vehicle within your visibility distance and free space in front of your vehicle.

Key Definitions

Trailer

Vehicle pulled by another vehicle or intended for that purpose. Includes semitrailers.

Passenger car

Vehicle with four or more wheels used to transport persons. No more than eight passenger seats (excl. driver). Does not include motor carriages with limited speed or disabled vehicles.

Commercial car

Vehicle with four or more wheels used to transport goods or carry out work. Not a motor carriage with limited speed or a disabled vehicle.

Van

Motor vehicle with a maximum authorised mass up to 3,500 kg, used to transport goods. Not a lorry.

Lorry

Motor vehicle with a maximum authorised mass of more than 3,500 kg, not equipped to transport persons. Used to transport goods.

Bus

Motor vehicle used to transport more than eight people, not including the driver.

T100 bus

Bus eligible for an increased maximum speed of 100 km/h, as noted on its registration certificate. Most have a circular sticker with "100" and a red border (e.g. coaches).

Public bus

Motor vehicle used for public transport.

Agricultural vehicle

Motor vehicle normally used for cultivation of land or transport of agricultural goods (e.g. tractor or combine). Legal speed limit is 40 km/h, depending on number plate, construction speed and road layout.

Erf (G-5)

Residential area where children play. Maximum speed: 15 km/h. Pedestrians may use the full width of the road inside an erf.

Inside built-up areas (H-1)

Area within the border of a village or city. Maximum speed: 50 km/h unless otherwise indicated.

Outside built-up areas (H-2)

Area outside the border of a village or city. Maximum speed: 80 km/h unless otherwise indicated.

Autoweg (G-3)

Speed limit: 100 km/h unless otherwise indicated. Parking spaces, petrol stations and bus stops are not part of the autoweg. One-carriageway autowegen are indicated by a green axis line.

Motorway (G-1)

Speed limit: 130 km/h unless otherwise indicated. Normally equipped with hard shoulders, which are usually not present on autowegen.

Along the autoweg or motorway there may be signs indicating a lowered speed limit — between certain times (e.g. 6 AM–7 PM) or as a fixed day-and-night reduction.
